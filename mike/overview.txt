KEY TIMING NOTES
----------------
- Early-to-mid May: fresh green maple season (shinryoku), rice paddy flooding, wisteria, azaleas, optimal hiking weather before June rains
- May 12 (Tue): Mikage Matsuri at Shimogamo Shrine — sacred Heian-era ritual, almost no tourists — DO NOT MISS
- May 14 (Thu): Smaller offering ritual at Shimogamo, 10am (travel day, skip unless afternoon train)
- May 15-17: Sanja Matsuri in Asakusa (2026 is ceremonial "Kagematsuri" year, no public mikoshi parade)
- Weekend in Tokyo (May 16-17): popular spots more crowded, nightlife/markets at their best

- Afternoon (2-5): one intentional thing (workshop, museum, garden)

- Dinner (7-9): small place
