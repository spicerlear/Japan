OPEN DECISIONS
--------------
1. Kumano Kodo (3-day pilgrimage hike May 11-13): yes or no? Costs Kyoto days, gains an epic hike.
2. Hakone serious hike (May 9): Mt. Kintoki (hard, Fuji views) or Tanzawa range (hardest, most remote)?
3. Tokyo mountain day (May 16): Mt. Mitake/Odake traverse or Mt. Kumotori (highest, hardest)?
4. Day-trip from Kyoto: Nara, Kurama/Kibune, or Uji? (if not doing Kumano Kodo)
5. Workshop pick: indigo dyeing, knife forging, cooking class, kintsugi, or tea ceremony?
6. Splurge meal: Kyoto kaiseki lunch, omakase sushi, or something else?
7. Tokyo cultural night: jazz, kabuki, or evening shrine?

DAILY PRIORITIES
----------------
- Early (6-9am): ONE famous thing while empty, OR sleep in
- Mid-morning (9-12): wander a neighborhood, breakfast, drift
- Lunch (12-2): real meal, sit down
- Afternoon (2-5): one intentional thing (workshop, museum, garden)
- Late afternoon (5-7): quiet-hours famous spot, or a long walk
- Dinner (7-9): small place
- Evening: bar, night walk, or collapse

KEY TIMING NOTES
----------------
- Early-to-mid May: fresh green maple season (shinryoku), rice paddy flooding, wisteria, azaleas, optimal hiking weather before June rains
- May 12: Mikage Matsuri at Shimogamo Shrine (sacred Heian-era ritual, almost no tourists) - DO NOT MISS
- May 15-17: Sanja Matsuri in Asakusa (2026 is ceremonial year, no public mikoshi parade)
- Weekend in Tokyo (May 16-17): popular spots more crowded, nightlife/markets at their best
