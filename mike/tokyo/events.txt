TOKYO - EVENTS
===============

Day 7 — Thu, May 14: Arrive Tokyo, Easy Evening
- Drop bags, don't be ambitious
- Small izakaya in a residential neighborhood — Sangenjaya or Yanaka for real-people feel
- Eat, drink, walk home, sleep

Day 8 — Fri, May 15: Yanaka + TeamLab Evening
- Morning: Yanaka — survived WWII bombing, pre-war wooden houses, narrow lanes, shrines, cemetery with cats
  - Yanaka Ginza street, cemetery, Nezu Shrine (azaleas blooming, quieter than famous shrines)
  - Lunch in Yanaka
- Afternoon: Rest, or Shinjuku Gyoen Garden (best garden in city, fresh May greenery, peaceful on weekday)
- Evening: TeamLab Planets or Borderless — BOOK FRIDAY EVENING SLOT IN ADVANCE
  - Planets = more sensory/intimate (barefoot, water rooms)
  - Borderless = more iconic

Day 9 — Sat, May 16: Senso-ji at Dawn + Kappabashi + Cultural Night
- 5:30am: Senso-ji at dawn — magical at 6am, mobbed by 9am
  - Walk Nakamise Street (closed up, beautifully empty)
  - Get the picture, get out. Breakfast in Asakusa.
- Walk to Kappabashi Street (between Asakusa and Ueno) — "kitchen town"
  - Handmade knives, ceramics, restaurant supply shops
  - This is where to buy the perfect knife
- Afternoon: Rest, or pick a craft/design neighborhood:
  - Shimokitazawa (vintage, used books, indie cafes)
  - Daikanyama / Nakameguro (design shops, Tsutaya bookstore, quiet canal walk)
- Evening — CULTURAL NIGHT (pick one):
  - Jazz basement bar — Cotton Club, Blue Note Tokyo, or Eagle in Yotsuya (listening bar, no talking, meditative)
  - Kabuki at Kabukiza in Ginza — single-act tickets Y1,000-2,500, English audio guides
  - Meiji Jingu before closing (~5pm in May) — forest path almost empty, profoundly peaceful. Pair with dinner in Harajuku/Omotesando.

Day 10 — Sun, May 17: Last Morning, Departure
- Slow breakfast, one final wander
- Daikanyama T-Site (Tsutaya bookstore) — beautiful Sunday morning for design-lovers
- OR Hama-rikyu Garden — waterside, traditional teahouse on pond, quiet Sunday morning
- Train to airport (flight ~4pm)

What to Skip:
- Tokyo Tower / Skytree — good views, not what you're after
- Akihabara — pop culture isn't your top interest
- Harajuku Takeshita Street — one of the most crowded streets, Saturday especially miserable
- Shibuya Crossing on Fri/Sat night — chaos. If curious, do it Sunday morning.

Crowd-Hack Calendar:
- Famous spots: before 8am or after 5pm (especially weekends)
- Museums and gardens: weekday mornings (only have Friday)
- Markets: at opening (Tsukiji outer market by 7am if you go)
- Restaurants on weekends: book ahead or arrive off-hours (5:30pm dinner, 1:30pm lunch)
