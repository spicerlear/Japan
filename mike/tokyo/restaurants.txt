TOKYO - RESTAURANTS
===================

- Small izakaya in Sangenjaya or Yanaka (arrival evening, real-people feel)
- Kappabashi Street (between Asakusa and Ueno) — handmade knives, ceramics, restaurant supply shops
- Tsukiji outer market by 7am (if you go)
- Weekend restaurants: book ahead or arrive off-hours (5:30pm dinner, 1:30pm lunch)
