TOKYO - RESTAURANTS (May 14-17)
===============================

DINNER May 14 (Thu) — Arrival Evening, Izakaya Night
  Aka Oni (Tabelog 3.57, sake izakaya, Sangenjaya, ~Y3,000-4,000)
    — 100+ sake varieties, 200+ rotating dishes. True locals' spot. Walk-in OK.
  ** Yakitori Tokoshima (Tabelog 3.72, yakitori, Nishi-Taishido, ~Y4,000-5,000) **
    — FIRE PICK. 1 stop from Sangenjaya, near your hotel. Reserve.
  Hantei (Tabelog 3.65, kushiage, Nezu, ~Y6,000-8,000 dinner / ~Y3,000 lunch)
    — Stunning Meiji-era 3-story Cultural Property building. Bib Gourmand. Reserve.

LUNCH May 15 (Fri) — Yanaka / Nezu
  Nezu Kamachiku (Tabelog 3.67, udon, Nezu, ~Y1,000-2,000)
    — Michelin Bib Gourmand. Historic stone warehouse. Handmade kamaage udon. Queue.
  Hantei (if not visited May 14 — lunch is more budget-friendly)

DINNER May 15 (Fri) — After TeamLab Planets (Toyosu area)
  ** Monja Tsukushiya Honten (Tabelog 3.80, monjayaki, Tsukishima, ~Y2,000-3,000) **
    — FIRE PICK. One of the highest-rated on Monja Street. Fun, interactive,
      quintessentially Tokyo. 1 subway stop from TeamLab Planets. Reserve.

BREAKFAST May 16 (Sat) — After 5:30am Senso-ji Dawn Walk
  Pelican Cafe (Tabelog 3.79 bakery, Tawaramachi, ~Y800-1,500)
    — Legendary bread since 1942. Thick-cut toast, grilled sandwiches.
      Opens 9am (not immediate post-Senso-ji — grab convenience store onigiri first).
  MISOJYU (miso soup + onigiri, near Senso-ji, ~Y1,000-1,500)
    — Opens 8am. Gorgeous miso soup bowls. Bridges gap before Pelican opens.

LUNCH May 16 (Sat) — Asakusa / Kappabashi Area
  ** Asakusa Hirayama (Tabelog 3.72, soba-kappo, near Kappabashi, ~Y2,000-4,000) **
    — FIRE PICK. Tabelog Top 100 Soba 2025. 100% buckwheat soba + Kaga vegetable
      tempura. Reserve via Tabelog.
  Namiki Yabu Soba (Tabelog 3.5+, traditional soba, Asakusa, ~Y1,000-2,000)
    — One of Tokyo's "Three Great Yabu Soba" since 1913. Walk-in, expect wait.

DINNER May 16 (Sat) — Cultural Night (depends on choice)
  If jazz (Yotsuya/Cotton Club): flexible neighborhood dining
  If kabuki (Ginza): Tonkatsu Aoki Ginza (~Y2,000-4,000, Tabelog Top 100 Tonkatsu)
  If Meiji Jingu (Harajuku): dinner in Omotesando after

BRUNCH May 17 (Sun) — Final Morning
  IVY PLACE (Tabelog 3.5+, brunch cafe, Daikanyama, ~Y1,500-2,500)
    — Beautiful leafy setting. Opens 7am. Pancakes, omelets, quality coffee.
      Reserve via TableCheck.
  Tsukiji Outer Market (5-10 min walk from Hama-rikyu, ~Y1,000-3,000)
    — Fresh sushi, tamagoyaki, street food. Vibrant morning food experience.

FIRE PICKS SUMMARY
  Yakitori Tokoshima — Tabelog 3.72, near hotel (May 14)
  Monja Tsukushiya — Tabelog 3.80, after TeamLab (May 15)
  Asakusa Hirayama — Tabelog 3.72, Top 100 Soba (May 16)

UNICORN (off-route but legendary)
  Tonkatsu Narikura (Tabelog 4.26, Minami-Asagaya, ~Y3,000-4,000)
    — #1 tonkatsu in Tokyo. Low-temperature fried, transcendent.
      Reservation-only via Omakase. If you can snag a slot, go.
