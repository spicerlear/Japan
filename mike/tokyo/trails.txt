MAY 16 HIKE DECISION: MITAKE/ODAKE TRAVERSE vs. MT. KUMOTORI
=============================================================

OPTION A: MT. MITAKE + MT. ODAKE TRAVERSE — RECOMMENDED
---------------------------------------------------------
Summit: Mt. Odake 1,267m (Mt. Mitake 929m)
Full traverse distance: ~11.5 km (Okutama Station → Odake → Rock Garden → Mitake → cable car down)
Elevation gain: ~860m
Duration: ~6 hours on trail
Difficulty: Moderate-Hard (serious day hike, not technical)

TRAIL CHARACTER
This is one of the best full-day hikes accessible from Tokyo. The route starts at Okutama
station and climbs through genuine mountain forest to Mt. Odake — a strenuous push with
rewarding views of the Okutama valley and, on clear days, Mt. Fuji. From Odake you traverse
the ridge to Mt. Mitake, then descend through the Rock Garden: a narrow forested valley of
moss-covered boulders, cascading streams, and two sacred waterfalls (Nanayo Falls 50m,
Ayahiro Falls 10m). End at the cable car or walk down through the ancient shrine village.

Summit of Mitake has a 1,200-year-old keyaki tree and Musashimitake-jinja Shrine — a living
mountain village of shrine priests who have lived here for generations.

MAY CONDITIONS
Peak season. Fresh shinryoku green, blooming shakunage rhododendrons on the approach to Odake,
moss luminous in spring humidity. Sources describe the Rock Garden in May as exceptional.
One of the best months to do this hike.

TRANSIT FROM TOKYO (very manageable)
JR Chuo Line (Shinjuku) → Tachikawa → JR Ome Line → Mitake Station (~90 min, ~¥1,000)
Bus from Mitake Station → Takimoto cable car station (10 min, ¥338)
First cable car: 7:30am

For the full traverse starting from Okutama:
JR Chuo Line → Tachikawa → JR Ome Line → Okutama Station (~100 min)
Leave Tokyo by 7:00am, on trail by 9:00am, finish by 3–4pm. Very doable.

CROWDS
Popular but not overwhelming. May 16 is a Saturday — start early. The Rock Garden draws
day-trippers but the Odake summit section thins out considerably. Nothing like a Golden Week
crowd. An early start has you on the Odake ridge before the cable car crowd arrives.

POST-HIKE
Mitake area has a riverside path and Mitake-san Onsen for a soak. Back in Tokyo by 6–7pm,
plenty of time for Tokyo's Saturday night.

VERDICT: The right call. A genuinely beautiful, varied day hike — strenuous enough to satisfy,
accessible enough to actually complete without heroics. May conditions are exceptional.
Complements the trip's nature-sacred-landscape profile well (ancient shrine village, old-growth
forest, sacred waterfalls).


OPTION B: MT. KUMOTORI (雲取山) — NOT RECOMMENDED AS A DAY TRIP
----------------------------------------------------------------
Summit: 2,017m (Tokyo's highest peak)
Full day-hike distance: ~20km (round trip from Kosode), OR 32km traverse (Mitsumine to Okutama)
Duration: 10+ hours on trail
Difficulty: Hard-Very Hard (fitness, not technical)

WHY IT'S PROBLEMATIC FOR MAY 16

1. THE LOGISTICS ARE BRUTAL
   Transit option A: Seibu Line from Ikebukuro → Seibu-Chichibu (90 min) + bus to Mitsumine
   (leaves 8:30–9:10, arrives trailhead ~10:25). You're starting a 10-hr hike at 10:30am.
   Transit option B: JR Ome Line → Okutama + bus to Kosode trailhead.
   Either way you need to leave Tokyo by 6:30–7:00am on a Saturday.

2. THE FULL TRAVERSE IS GENUINELY DANGEROUS AS A DAY TRIP
   The 32km Mitsumine-to-Okutama traverse is what a serious source (4corners7seas) describes
   as requiring exceptional fitness — and explicitly advises against attempting in one day.
   The Ishione ridge section has multiple steep ascents and descents that grind you down late.
   The author finished it but called it dangerous and said he wouldn't recommend it.

3. BETTER AS AN OVERNIGHT
   Kumotori-so mountain hut at the summit makes this a beautiful 2-night trip. As a day hike,
   you're rushing a mountain that deserves to be savored. The 2,017m summit view is the reward
   — but you're too exhausted to appreciate it.

4. THE PAYOFF DIFFERENCE
   Kumotori is higher and more remote, but the forest and ridge character are not dramatically
   more beautiful than the Odake approach. The Rock Garden on the Mitake route is genuinely
   more scenic and varied than the Kumotori trail.

WHEN KUMOTORI MAKES SENSE: Overnight trip in late July/August with long daylight, staying at
the Kumotori-so hut. A future trip, not this one.
