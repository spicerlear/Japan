## Key Timing Note
Early-to-mid May is arguably the *best* window for deep-nature Japan: fresh green maple season (*shinryoku*), rice paddy flooding/reflection, wisteria, azaleas, optimal hiking weather before June rains. Cherry blossoms are over but tradeoff is worth it.

## The Big Structural Question: Kumano Kodo

The Kumano Kodo pilgrimage trail (Kii Peninsula, UNESCO World Heritage alongside Camino de Santiago) is described as "almost designed for" my profile: 1,000+ year old trails through cryptomeria forests, moss-covered stone paths, tiny mountain villages with rice terraces, *oji* subsidiary shrines, three grand shrines (Kumano Sanzan), low crowds. Classic Nakahechi route is 3 days / ~38km serious hiking. Would carve days out of Kyoto stay (May 11–13).

## Logistics Notes
- **Luggage forwarding (*takkyubin*):** Send big bags from airport directly to Kyoto hotel (~¥2,000/bag, 1–2 days, extremely reliable). Highly recommended for the May 7 transit-on-arrival day — just carry a small overnight bag to Hakone.
- **Airport question still open:** Haneda vs Narita affects the route to Hakone (Haneda much easier; Narita adds ~1 hr).
- **Reservations needed:** Saihō-ji moss garden, Shugakuin Imperial Villa, and Kumano Kodo *minshuku* all book up in advance.
