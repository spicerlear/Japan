MAY 9 HIKE DECISION: MT. KINTOKI vs. TANZAWA
=============================================

OPTION A: MT. KINTOKI (金時山) — RECOMMENDED
---------------------------------------------
Summit: 1,212m
Distance: 6.3 km loop
Elevation gain: 517m
Duration: 3.5–4 hours (comfortably done by midday)
Difficulty: Moderate (Ridgeline: 2/10 — "relatively easy for the moderately fit")
AllTrails rating: Hard (reflects the final chain-aided scramble, not the overall trail)

TRAIL CHARACTER
The approach climbs through cedar forest to an exposed summit with one of the most iconic
close-range views of Mt. Fuji in Japan — you're looking at it across the whole Hakone caldera.
The final push is a scramble aided by fixed chains/ladders (fun, not technical). Two teahouses
at the summit sell noodles and snacks.

FUJI VIEWS
Reliable on clear days. This is one of the best Fuji viewpoints in Japan — close, unobstructed,
with Lake Ashi visible below. May morning skies are typically clearest before midday cloud build.
Start early for the best chance.

TRANSIT FROM HAKONE (very easy — you're already there)
Hakone Tozan Bus Route L from Hakone-Yumoto Station → Kintoki Jinja Iriguchi (24 min, ¥840)
Or taxi/car from your accommodation. This is a major advantage over Tanzawa.

CROWDS
Popular on weekends. May 9 is a Saturday — expect other hikers on the trail and a busy summit.
Start by 7–7:30am to beat the main crowd and get the best Fuji views before cloud rolls in.
The summit teahouses get full by mid-morning. An early start largely solves the crowd problem.

MAY CONDITIONS
Excellent. Pre-rainy season, fresh green foliage, azaleas blooming on the approach. Cool temps
at the summit (bring a layer). Trail in good condition.

POST-HIKE
Back in Hakone by midday. Afternoon free for onsen, Hakone Open-Air Museum, or relaxed
exploration. Good use of a full Hakone day.

VERDICT: The right call for your trip context. You're staying in Hakone, transit is trivial,
it's a genuine mountain experience with one of Japan's iconic views, and you're done by noon.


OPTION B: TANZAWA RANGE (丹沢山) — NOT RECOMMENDED FOR MAY 9
-------------------------------------------------------------
Summit: Mt. Tanzawa 1,567m / Mt. Hirugatake 1,673m
Distance: 23.7km (full traverse — this is a 2-day mountain)
Day-hike option: Yabitsu Pass → Mt. Tanzawa, ~8 miles, 3,877 ft gain, 6.5–7.5 hrs on trail
Difficulty: Hard

WHY IT'S PROBLEMATIC FOR MAY 9

1. LEECHES — significant issue
   Land leeches are active in the Tanzawa range from May through October. They climb up
   from the ground onto boots and socks and bite. Salt/repellent available at trailhead
   visitor center but this is a real nuisance on a hot day. May is the start of leech season.

2. TRANSIT IS COMPLICATED
   Yabitsu Pass (best day-hike trailhead) is accessed via bus from Isehara or Hadano station
   on the Odakyu Line — not from Hakone. You'd need to backtrack toward Tokyo, change lines,
   and hope bus timing works. No direct route from Hakone accommodations. Transit alone eats
   1.5–2 hrs each way.

3. DURATION
   A proper Tanzawa day hike is 6.5–8 hrs on trail. Add the transit complexity and you're
   looking at a 12–14 hr door-to-door day. Miserable and leaves no margin for error.

4. VIEWS
   Fuji views exist but are less dramatic than Kintoki — you're deeper in the range, surrounded
   by ridgeline rather than perched above the Hakone caldera. Less photogenic payoff.

WHEN TANZAWA MAKES SENSE: Weekday in April or late September, Tokyo-based, accept the leeches,
camping at Miyama-sansou hut for a proper 2-day ridge traverse. Not your situation.
