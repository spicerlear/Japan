HAKONE - EVENTS
================

Day 1 — Thu, May 7: Arrival
- Arrive Hakone ~8pm (train from Shinjuku, tickets booked)
- Onsen evening at ryokan
- Kaiseki dinner at ryokan
- Pure recovery day — rest

Day 2 — Fri, May 8: Cultural-Botanical Day
- Old Tokaido cedar avenue walk (historic Edo-era road lined with giant cedars)
- Hakone Wetlands Botanical Garden (Sengokuhara area, rare flora)
- Onsen evening

Day 3 — Sat, May 9: Serious Hike OR Departure
- Option A: Mt. Kintoki hike (hard, Fuji views on clear day)
- Option B: Tanzawa range deep hike (hardest, most remote)
- Morning: Sengokuhara pampas plateau or Owakudani volcanic area (if departing early)
- Train to Kyoto (~2.5 hrs shinkansen)

Ongoing:
- Onsen evenings throughout stay
- Hakone 3-Day Pass: PURCHASED (covers transit, some attractions)
