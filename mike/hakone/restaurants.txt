HAKONE - RESTAURANTS (May 7-9)
==============================

DINNER May 7 (Thu) — Arrival Night
  Kaiseki at ryokan (already planned)
  Backup: Mori Meshi (Tabelog 3.54, izakaya/seafood, Miyanoshita, ~Y3,000-4,000)

LUNCH May 8 (Fri) — Exploring Hakone Loop
  Tamura Ginkatsutei (Tabelog 3.50, tofu katsu nabe, Gora, ~Y1,000-2,000)
    — Iconic: ground pork between tofu slices, breaded, fried, simmered. Arrive before 11am.
  Takeyabu (Tabelog 3.69, soba, Motohakone/Lake Ashi, ~Y1,000-2,000)
    — Tabelog Top 100 Soba 5 years running. 22 seats, cash only.
  Amazake Chaya (Tabelog 3.67, teahouse, Old Tokaido Road, ~Y1,000)
    — 400-year-old teahouse. Amazake, fresh mochi, miso oden. Not a full meal.

DINNER May 8 (Fri)
  Mori Meshi (Tabelog 3.54, izakaya/seafood, Miyanoshita, ~Y3,000-4,000)
    — Best izakaya in Hakone. Local Odawara seafood, oden, mountain views.
  Yoraku (Tabelog 3.54, tai-meshi, Tonosawa, ~Y3,000-5,000)
    — Sea bream rice specialist. River views, terrace seating. Reserve online only.

LUNCH May 9 (Sat) — Before Departing for Kyoto
  Yubadon Naokichi (Tabelog 3.49, yuba donburi, Hakone-Yumoto, ~Y1,000-2,000)
    — Spring-water yuba. 3 min from station. Queue expected.
  Hatsu Hana (Tabelog 3.49, natural yam soba, Hakone-Yumoto, ~Y1,000-2,000)
    — Hakone's most famous soba. Invented natural yam soba. Huge queues.

FIRE PICK (worth a detour)
  Menan Chitose (Tabelog 3.79, ramen, Kazamatsuri/Odawara, ~Y1,000-2,000)
    — Highest-rated eatery on Hakone route. Top 100 Ramen. 15 seats, lunch only
      (11:30-14:30). 1 stop before Hakone-Yumoto. Could hit on arrival or departure day.

NOTE: Many Hakone restaurants close Wednesday. Your dates (Thu-Sat) are safe.
