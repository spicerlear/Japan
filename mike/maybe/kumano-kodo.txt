MAYBE: KUMANO KODO PILGRIMAGE (3-4 day hike)
==============================================

WHAT IT IS
----------
Kumano Kodo — UNESCO World Heritage pilgrimage trail on the Kii Peninsula (alongside Camino de Santiago). 1,000+ year old trails through cryptomeria forests, moss-covered stone paths, tiny mountain villages with rice terraces, oji subsidiary shrines, three grand shrines (Kumano Sanzan). Low crowds.

THE ROUTE: NAKAHECHI (Takijiri to Hongu)
-----------------------------------------
~38-40km through the mountains. Two options:

3-day version (aggressive):
  Day 1: Takijiri-oji to Takahara (~3.5 hrs, steep climb)
  Day 2: Takahara to Nonaka/Chikatsuyu (~7 hrs, full day)
  Day 3: Nonaka to Kumano Hongu Taisha (~7 hrs, full day)

4-day version (more comfortable, recommended):
  Day 1: Takijiri-oji to Takahara (half day, steep but short)
  Day 2: Takahara to Chikatsuyu (~6 hrs, difficulty 3.5/5)
  Day 3: Chikatsuyu/Nonaka to Kumano Hongu Taisha (~7 hrs, difficulty 4/5)
  Day 4: Yunomine Onsen + return to Kyoto

OVERNIGHT STOPS
---------------
Night 1: TAKAHARA
  - Small ridgeline settlement with panoramic views of Hatenashi mountains
  - Takahara Kumano-jinja shrine (one of the oldest on the route)
  - Family-run minshuku

Night 2: CHIKATSUYU or NONAKA
  - River valley village (Chikatsuyu) or near Tsugizakura-oji spring (Nonaka)
  - A few small family-run guesthouses
  - Chikatsuyu also has Iris Park campground if preferred

Night 3: YUNOMINE ONSEN
  - 1,800 years old, only UNESCO-listed hot spring in the world
  - Post-hike soak — the reward at the end

ACCOMMODATION: MINSHUKU (recommended)
--------------------------------------
- ~7,300-15,600 yen/night per person
- Dinner + breakfast INCLUDED (served at fixed times)
- Most require minimum 2 guests (works perfectly for you two)
- Rooms are very limited — tiny villages with only a handful of guesthouses each
- Must communicate: arrival time, dietary needs, allergies when booking

ACCOMMODATION: CAMPGROUNDS (budget option)
-------------------------------------------
- Designated campgrounds at Chikatsuyu (Iris Park), Kawayu Onsen, Koguchi
- ~1,600-2,000 yen/night
- First-come first-served, no reservation needed
- No open fires allowed; gas stoves OK
- NOTE: Wild/free camping is PROHIBITED on the Nakahechi route

HOW TO BOOK
------------
Official system: KUMANO TRAVEL (kumano-travel.com)
- Community reservation system run by Tanabe City Tourism Bureau
- Pick itinerary, enter start date, choose from available accommodation at each stop
- Also handles luggage forwarding between villages (hike with daypacks only)
- English support available

ACTION REQUIRED: Book ASAP — May is peak season, rooms are scarce.

WHAT IT COSTS THE PLAN
-----------------------
- Takes 3-4 days out of Kyoto (May 11-13 or 11-14)
- Loses: Arashiyama day, day-trip (Nara/Kurama/Uji), possibly the workshop day
- Kyoto reduced to: arrival evening + Higashiyama dawn + Mikage Matsuri day + departure morning
- If 4-day version: return to Kyoto evening of May 14 = loses Tokyo arrival evening too

WHAT IT GAINS
-------------
- The single best match for your shared hiking/nature/sacred-space profile
- Multi-day immersive hike through ancient forest
- Minshuku stays (authentic village accommodation)
- Yunomine Onsen (UNESCO hot spring)
- Cryptomeria forests, moss paths, mountain villages, rice terraces
