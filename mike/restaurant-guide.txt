RESTAURANT FILTERING GUIDE — TABELOG EDITION
=============================================

HOW TABELOG SCORES WORK
-----------------------
Tabelog scores are MUCH harsher than Google/Yelp. Recalibrate your brain:

  3.0-3.49  = Decent (would be 4.0+ on Google)
  3.5-3.59  = Good — worth going to
  3.6-3.69  = Very good — locals recommend these
  3.7-3.79  = Excellent — destination-worthy
  3.8+      = Exceptional — often reservation-only, weeks ahead
  4.0+      = Unicorn territory — maybe 50 restaurants in all of Japan

For your mid-range, slow-wandering style: target 3.5-3.7 as your sweet spot.
Above 3.7 often means formal, expensive, and hard to book last-minute.


YOUR FILTERING CRITERIA
-----------------------
For each restaurant you're considering, score it against these:

1. TABELOG SCORE: 3.5+ minimum (skip anything below unless a local told you)
2. PROXIMITY: Is it walkable from that day's planned neighborhood?
3. PRICE: Lunch under Y3,000 / Dinner under Y6,000 (except the one splurge)
4. VIBE FIT: Small, unpretentious, local feel (your stated preference)
5. TIMING: Can you hit it at an off-peak hour? (5:30pm dinner, 11:30am lunch)
6. RESERVATION: Does it need one? Can you book via Tabelog or walk in?

Quick decision: If a place scores 3.5+ AND is near your route AND fits
your price range — it goes on the shortlist. Everything else is noise.

HOW TO SEARCH TABELOG EFFICIENTLY
---------------------------------
1. Go to tabelog.com (use Chrome translate or the English toggle)
2. Search by area name in Japanese for better results:
     Gion = 祇園  |  Pontocho = 先斗町  |  Arashiyama = 嵐山
     Yanaka = 谷中  |  Asakusa = 浅草  |  Sangenjaya = 三軒茶屋
     Shimogamo = 下鴨  |  Kappabashi = 合羽橋
3. Filter by cuisine (和食 = Japanese, 居酒屋 = izakaya, 蕎麦 = soba, etc.)
4. Sort by rating (ランキング)
5. Check: dinner budget (夜の予算) and lunch budget (昼の予算)
6. Look at photo reviews — Japanese reviewers post detailed food photos

Tip: Tabelog's "百名店" (Hyakumeiten / Top 100) lists are curated yearly
awards by cuisine. Search "tabelog 百名店 2025 [cuisine]" for pre-filtered
excellence.

DECISION RULES
--------------
When your shortlist has 3+ options for the same meal slot:
- Pick the highest Tabelog score that fits your route
- Break ties with: walk-in > reservation, cheaper > pricier, smaller > bigger
- Keep 1 backup per meal in case your first pick has a line

You do NOT need to pre-book every meal. Only book:
- The splurge kaiseki (book ASAP, 2-4 weeks ahead minimum)
- Any dinner at a place with Tabelog 3.7+ (likely needs reservation)
- Everything else: walk in at off-peak times and you'll be fine
