KYOTO - EVENTS
===============

- May 12 (Tue): Mikage Matsuri at Shimogamo Shrine, from 9am — sacred Heian-era procession + bugaku dance in Tadasu no Mori. DO NOT MISS.
- May 14 (Thu): Smaller offering ritual at Shimogamo Shrine, 10am (travel day to Tokyo, skip unless afternoon shinkansen)

Workshop options (pick one, ~2-3 hrs):
- Indigo (aizome) dyeing
- Knife forging or sharpening at a Kyoto bladesmith
- Kyoto-style cooking class (small home setting)
- Kintsugi (golden-joinery pottery repair)
- Tea ceremony (Camellia Garden or En)

Shopping/craft:
- Kaikado (handmade tea caddies, 150+ years)
- Asahi-yaki ceramics
- Antique shops of Shinmonzen Street
