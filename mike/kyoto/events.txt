KYOTO - EVENTS
===============

Day 1 — Sat, May 9: Arrival & Easing In
- Arrive from Hakone afternoon
- Wander Gion and Pontocho at dusk (lantern-lit alleys, Kamogawa river)
- Dinner at a small place along the river
- Bed early

Day 2 — Sun, May 10: Higashiyama at Dawn
- 5:30am alarm — walk Higashiyama temple lanes (Sannenzaka, Ninenzaka, up to Kiyomizu-dera) while empty
- By 9am tourist flood arrives; be eating breakfast by then
- Afternoon: Maruyama Park or Chion-in temple (huge, impressive, quieter than Kiyomizu)
- CROWD NOTE: Sunday is worst for famous sites. Sunrise is the only way.

Day 3 — Mon, May 11: Arashiyama Day
- Bamboo grove by 7am (crowd rule)
- Okochi Sanso Villa (small fee, matcha + sweet, hidden gem garden)
- Arashiyama town for lunch, river, Togetsukyo Bridge
- Afternoon: Sagano Romantic Train (if running) OR uphill temples — Jojakko-ji, Gio-ji (moss garden, gorgeous, quiet)
- Train back for dinner

Day 4 — Tue, May 12: MIKAGE MATSURI + Northern Kyoto [DO NOT MISS]
- 8:30am: Arrive Shimogamo Shrine for Mikage Matsuri procession + bugaku dance (starts 9am)
- Sacred Heian-era ritual in 1,000-year-old forest, almost no tourists
- After: Stroll Tadasu no Mori (primeval forest, wandering paths)
- Daitoku-ji temple complex (zen sub-temples, rock gardens) — pick 2-3: Daisen-in, Koto-in, Ryogen-in
- End day at Kamigamo Shrine (other end of Aoi route, quiet)

Day 5 — Wed, May 13: Day-Trip OR Workshop Day
DAY-TRIP OPTIONS (pick one):
- Nara (1 hr train): Todai-ji giant Buddha, Kasuga Taisha lantern path, Naramachi old merchant district, deer
- Kurama & Kibune (45 min train): Hike from Kurama-dera over mountain to Kibune (~2 hrs, gentle). Almost no foreign tourists. Best for nature + slow wandering.
- Uji (20 min train): Matcha capital, tea fields, Byodo-in temple (on the Y10 coin), grind your own matcha. Half-day.

WORKSHOP OPTIONS (pick one, ~2-3 hrs):
- Indigo (aizome) dyeing — make your own dyed cloth
- Knife forging or sharpening at a Kyoto bladesmith
- Kyoto-style cooking class (small home setting)
- Kintsugi (golden-joinery pottery repair) — meditative, make something together
- Tea ceremony at a real tea house (Camellia Garden or En)

(Can do BOTH: half-day workshop + half-day Uji or Nara)

Day 6 — Thu, May 14: Final Kyoto Morning, Travel to Tokyo
- Early walk: Philosopher's Path (canal-side, tree-lined, connects Ginkaku-ji to Nanzen-ji)
- Nanzen-ji aqueduct (Meiji-era brick aqueduct through temple complex)
- Lunch near Kyoto Station
- Shinkansen to Tokyo

Anytime Picks (sprinkle throughout):
- Nishiki Market — narrow covered food street. Early lunch on weekday, eat your way through.
- Kaikado (handmade tea caddies, 150+ years), Asahi-yaki ceramics, antique shops of Shinmonzen Street
- Fushimi Inari at sunset — thousand red torii. By day = selfies; at dusk = empty, lanterns on. Up to Yotsutsuji (~30 min) for city view.
- A real tea ceremony (if not done as workshop)

Splurge Meal:
- Kyoto kaiseki LUNCH (same chef, half the dinner price, Y8,000-15,000/person)
- Options: Gion Karyo, Giro Giro Hitoshina (informal, modern), Honke Owariya (ancient soba since 1465)

What to Skip:
- Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) — stand in a herd, look at building across pond. If must go, opening time only.
- Gion geisha-spotting tours — touristy/uncomfortable. Do geisha culture via meal or tea ceremony instead.
- Bus tours of any kind

May 14 (Thu) note: Smaller offering ritual at Shimogamo Shrine, 10am — skip unless afternoon shinkansen
