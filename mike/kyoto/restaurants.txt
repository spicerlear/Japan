KYOTO - RESTAURANTS
====================

Splurge meal (lunch kaiseki recommended - same craft, half the price):
- Gion Karyo
- Giro Giro Hitoshina (informal, modern)
- Honke Owariya (ancient soba, running since 1465)

Anytime:
- Nishiki Market — narrow covered food street, pickles, dashi rolls, fish cakes, matcha sweets (early lunch on weekday)
- Small unpretentious place along Kamogawa river (arrival evening)

Day-trip options:
- Naramachi (Nara) — quiet old merchant district
- Uji — matcha capital, tea fields, grind your own matcha
