KYOTO - RESTAURANTS (May 9-14)
==============================

DINNER May 9 (Sat) — Arrival, Gion/Pontocho/Kamogawa
  Oryori Menami (Tabelog 3.55, obanzai, Sanjo Kiyamachi, ~Y3,000-5,000)
    — Michelin Bib Gourmand. Kyoto home cooking since 1939. Walk-in OK.
  Pontocho Masuda (obanzai/izakaya, Pontocho alley, ~Y4,000-5,000)
    — Michelin Guide 2025. Showa-era atmosphere. Father-son ryotei-trained team.

BREAKFAST May 10 (Sun) — After 5:30am Higashiyama Dawn Walk
  ** Choshoku Kishin (Tabelog 3.70, Japanese breakfast, Gion, ~Y3,000-4,000) **
    — FIRE PICK. Michelin Bib Gourmand. Fresh yuba, 3 soups, unlimited rice.
      ONLY 8 SEATS. Reserve far in advance. Perfect post-dawn-walk reward.

LUNCH May 10 (Sun) — Higashiyama / Nanzen-ji Area
  Hinode Udon (Tabelog Top 100 Udon, curry udon, Keage, ~Y1,000-1,500)
    — Famous 15-spice bonito dashi curry udon. Walk-in, short queue. English menu.
  Okutan Nanzenji (yudofu, Nanzen-ji grounds, ~Y3,000-4,200)
    — Operating since 1635. Thatched-roof temple garden. Tofu boiled at your table.

LUNCH May 11 (Mon) — Arashiyama
  Shoraian (tofu kaiseki, past bamboo grove, ~Y3,800-5,800)
    — #1 tofu restaurant in Arashiyama. Mountain/river views from dining room.
      Creative tofu dishes. Reserve ahead.

LUNCH May 12 (Tue) — Northern Kyoto / Shimogamo / Daitoku-ji
  Izusen at Daiji-in (shojin ryori, inside Daitoku-ji, ~Y3,000+)
    — Zen Buddhist vegetarian in temple garden. Nested lacquer bowls. 11am-4pm.
  ** Ogawa (Tabelog 3.72, soba, near Kitayama Station, ~Y1,000-2,000) **
    — FIRE PICK. Michelin 1-star. 4 tables, 12 seats, lunch only.
      On your northern Kyoto route. Hidden gem.

ANYTIME / FLEXIBLE
  Inoda Coffee Honten (kissaten, Karasuma Oike, ~Y1,000-1,500)
    — Kyoto's most iconic kissaten since 1947. "Kyoto Breakfast" set. Walk-in.
  Omen Ginkakuji (udon, near Silver Pavilion, ~Y1,000-1,500)
    — Kyoto institution. Thick handmade udon with seasonal vegetable dipping tray.

LUNCH May 14 (Thu) — Final Kyoto Meal, Near Station
  Shinpuku Saikan (Tabelog 3.59, dark soy ramen, Kyoto Station, ~Y700-1,000)
    — Legendary ramen right at Kyoto Station. 4,094 reviews. Walk-in.
  (Honke Daiichi Asahi next door is rated 3.74 but MAY be closed Thursdays — verify)

SPLURGE KAISEKI — Pick One
  ** Aji Fukushima (Tabelog 3.78, kappo/kaiseki, Gion, lunch ~Y7,000) **
    — FIRE PICK. Michelin star within 1.5 years. Top splurge candidate.
      Dinner obanzai dishes from Y500 each (also works as a non-splurge dinner).
      Lunch Tue-Sat 11:30-13:00. Closed Sun.
  Giro Giro Hitoshina (Tabelog 3.6+, punk kaiseki, Kiyomizu-Gojo, ~Y5,000-6,000)
    — Creative modern kaiseki, counter seating, open kitchen. CASH ONLY.
      Must book via JPNEAZY or in person. Hard to reserve.
  Gion Karyo (Tabelog ~3.5, traditional kaiseki, Gion, lunch from ~Y5,000)
    — 30+ year history. Bookable via TableCheck. Most traditional option.

FIRE PICKS SUMMARY
  Choshoku Kishin — Tabelog 3.70, breakfast, 8 seats (May 10)
  Ogawa — Tabelog 3.72, Michelin soba, 12 seats (May 12)
  Aji Fukushima — Tabelog 3.78, Michelin kappo (splurge)
