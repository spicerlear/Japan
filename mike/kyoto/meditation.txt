KYOTO - MEDITATION / TEMPLES
==============================

- Shimogamo Shrine — Mikage Matsuri May 12, 9am (sacred bugaku dance in Tadasu no Mori ancient forest)
- Daitoku-ji temple complex — cluster of zen sub-temples with rock gardens (Daisen-in, Koto-in, Ryogen-in)
- Kamigamo Shrine — quiet, lovely
- Chion-in temple — huge, impressive, quieter than Kiyomizu
- Nanzen-ji — Meiji-era brick aqueduct through temple complex
- Ginkaku-ji (via Philosopher's Path)
- Saiho-ji moss garden — 120 species of moss (reservation required)
- Shugakuin Imperial Villa — borrowed-landscape garden (advance reservation, nearly empty)

If doing Kumano Kodo (May 11-13):
- Kumano Hongu Taisha (grand shrine)
- Oji subsidiary shrines along Nakahechi route
- Yunomine Onsen (1,800 years old, UNESCO-listed hot spring)
