KYOTO - HIKING TRAILS
======================

City walks:
- Higashiyama lanes at dawn (Sannenzaka, Ninenzaka, up to Kiyomizu-dera) — 5:30am start, empty before 9am
- Philosopher's Path (canal-side, Ginkaku-ji to Nanzen-ji)
- Fushimi Inari at sunset — up to Yotsutsuji (~30 min up) for city view

Arashiyama (May 11 if not doing Kumano Kodo):
- Bamboo grove at 7am
- Okochi Sanso Villa (hidden garden, includes matcha)
- Uphill temples: Jojakko-ji, Gio-ji (moss garden)

Day-trip hikes (May 13 if not doing Kumano Kodo):
- Kurama to Kibune mountain traverse (~2 hrs, gentle, sacred cedar forest)
- Ohara valley (Sanzen-in temple, hike up through Jakko-in)
- Kitayama cedar forest (600-year-old forestry craft)

Kumano Kodo (May 11-13 if yes):
- Nakahechi route: Takijiri trailhead to Kumano Hongu Taisha (~38km, 3 days)
- Cryptomeria forests, moss-covered stone paths, tiny mountain villages
- Stay in family-run minshuku, soak in Yunomine Onsen
